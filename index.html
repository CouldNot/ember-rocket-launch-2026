COMPONENT INSPECTOR

WHAT IT TRACKS

Particles near the breathing zone.

The wearable samples nearby air to record PM2.5 concentration over time and location. The SPS30 uses laser scattering to estimate particle concentrations; the dashboard builds an exposure history.

Environmental exposure, not measured lung deposition. This sensor does not identify chemicals, radioactivity, or health status.

DATA / FROM AIR TO DASHBOARD

Exactly what gets recorded.

The prototype reads PM2.5 every second and sends a summarized record every 10 seconds.

PRIMARY SENSOR OUTPUTPM2.5 µg/m³pm25_ug_m3

Mass concentration of fine particles in nearby air. Missing or failed readings remain null or invalid.

Additional SPS30 outputs available for future integration

The hardware also reports mass concentrations for PM1.0, PM4 and PM10 (µg/m³), number concentrations for PM0.5, PM1.0, PM2.5, PM4 and PM10 (#/cm³), and typical particle size (µm). These are not fields in the current prototype record. PM4 and PM10 values are calculated from the particle distribution profile.

The dashboard’s exposure index is calculated separately as Σ(PM2.5 × elapsed minutes), in µg·min/m³. It is not a sensor-measured medical dose.
