WHAT TRIGGERS THE DEMO ALERT?

A sustained rise, then a check-in.

After 15 consecutive valid one-second samples at or above this threshold, the device flags an alert. The next 10-second packet delivers it to the phone. The figure then leaves the perimeter.

Illustrative rule only—not an occupational limit or a real evacuation decision. Operational thresholds and withdrawal instructions require incident-command validation. Lower readings outside the perimeter do not establish that it is safe to return.

Demo alert timing is calculated from the trajectory.
Exact data fields and simulation assumptions

The prototype record contains device_id, firefighter_id, timestamp, latitude, longitude, pm25_ug_m3, co_ppm, radiation_uSv_h, source, quality. This demonstration uses a fictional local route instead of real GPS coordinates. CO and radiation are null because separate sensors are not fitted. It does not measure temperature, heart rate, oxygen, blood chemistry, lung capacity, or deposited lung mass.

Smoke concentration follows a scripted spatial gradient, not a physical wildfire model. The exposure index integrates one-second PM2.5 samples over elapsed time. Phone cards show the most recent 10-second summary, so they intentionally lag the sensor cards. Changing the threshold or scrubbing restarts acknowledgement state.
